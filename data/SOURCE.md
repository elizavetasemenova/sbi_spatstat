# Data provenance

`BF_malaria_data.csv` — Burkina Faso malaria parasite-rate survey data
(examined / positives / longitude / latitude at 109 survey clusters).
Obtained from the open teaching repository for the "Spatial epidemiology in R"
course by H. Sturrock (github.com/HughSt/HughSt.github.io), which compiles
publicly released parasite-rate survey data of the type curated by the Malaria
Atlas Project. Used here only to demonstrate the inference method; no
epidemiological claims are made.
